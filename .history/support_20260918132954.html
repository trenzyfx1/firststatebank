Quick answers

Why is my withdrawal pending?+

Pending withdrawals are usually waiting on approval or verification. Most clear within a few hours.

How do I reset my payment code?+

Go to Settings → Security & Payment Code. Enter your current code, then choose a new 4-digit code.

A transfer failed — what now?+

Failed transfers are automatically reversed within 24 hours. If it's not back, open a ticket with the reference number.
