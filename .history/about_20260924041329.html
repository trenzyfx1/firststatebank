About Us

A few years ago, a small team set out to make banking feel less like a queue and more like a relationship. That work became First State Bank. Today we're a fully licensed bank with tools for tracking spending, saving more and making the right money moves — without overcharging you for the privilege.

Wherever you are and whatever stage your business is at, we're built to make the day-to-day parts of banking easier, so you can spend your time on everything else.

To give every customer a safe, unique and secure platform for their everyday finances, backed by an infrastructure built for convenience.

To be recognised as the bank that treats every customer — regardless of size — like a long-term relationship, not a transaction.

A brief history about us

First State Bank was founded in 2011 by a group of community-minded bankers who saw the need for accessible financial services for individuals and small businesses that larger banks tended to overlook. They believed that by offering approachable loans and everyday accounts, they could support local economic growth.

In the early days, First State Bank focused on small loans for local entrepreneurs. Demand quickly outpaced a single branch, and new locations followed in neighbouring cities.

As the bank grew, the product range widened to include savings accounts, insurance and money transfers, alongside mobile banking that made it easier for customers to reach us from anywhere.

A growth investment in 2015 allowed the bank to expand into new markets while keeping the branch-level relationships that customers had come to expect. First State Bank has stayed guided by that same mission ever since: helping people and businesses bank on their own terms.
