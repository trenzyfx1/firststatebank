About Us

A bank that still remembers your name.

First State Bank started with a small team who believed banking had gotten too impersonal. What began as a single branch savings counter has grown into a full-service bank — without losing the habit of picking up the phone when you call.

Today we combine dependable, well-regulated banking with the kind of modern tools you'd expect: mobile transfers, spend tracking and same-day support, all backed by people who know your account history, not just your account number.

Our Story

A brief history

First State Bank was founded in 2011 by a small group of local bankers who saw too many individuals and small businesses underserved by larger institutions. They started with straightforward savings accounts and small-business loans in a single office.

As demand grew, so did our branch network. We began offering savings products, insurance and money transfers, followed by mobile banking so customers could reach us without visiting a branch.

In 2015, a growth investment let us expand into new markets while keeping the same relationship-first approach.
